🛡️ Official Privacy Policy

USB Disk Utility & Storage Suite

Effective Date: August 24, 2026 • Version: 1.0

1. Summary & Core Privacy Commitment

Zero Data Collection Policy: USB Disk Utility does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, files, disk contents, or sensitive device data. All storage operations execute 100% locally and offline on your device.

This Privacy Policy applies to the USB Disk Utility & Storage Suite (package name: com.clustereddreams.usbdisk.app) developed by Clustered Dreams. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right, especially when managing personal files and low-level physical storage drives.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

When you use USB Disk Utility, we have no access to:

  • Disk Contents & User Files: Your files, partitions, photos, videos, documents, or recovered data never leave your device.
  • Personal Identity & Accounts: We do not require registration, login, email addresses, names, or phone numbers.
  • Location Data: We do not request, track, or collect GPS or network location.
  • Contacts, Microphone, Camera: We do not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • Advertising Identifiers: The application contains zero advertisements and zero advertising tracking SDKs.

3. Local Device & USB Permissions

The application requests strictly necessary system permissions solely for local hardware interaction:

  • USB Host Access (android.hardware.usb.host): Required to communicate directly with attached USB OTG flash drives, SSDs, SD card readers, and mass storage devices via standard SCSI/BOT protocols. USB device communication occurs strictly between Android and the physical drive.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): Used to prevent Android from prematurely terminating long-running disk operations (such as formatting, flashing ISO images, counterfeit scans, or file carving) while the phone screen is off or in the background.
  • Wake Lock: Prevents the CPU from sleeping while an active write/erase operation is in progress, ensuring physical drive partitions are not corrupted by sudden interruptions.

4. Anonymous Crash Diagnostics & Analytics

To diagnose unexpected crashes, SCSI protocol anomalies, and device compatibility issues across diverse Android hardware, the app may collect anonymous diagnostic information via Google Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Device Model & OS Version (e.g., Pixel 10 Pro Fold, Android 17).
  • Crash Stack Traces (technical error codes and exception details).
  • Non-Personal Hardware Attributes (e.g., USB Vendor ID and Product ID of connected card readers).

This diagnostic information contains no personally identifiable information (PII), is encrypted in transit via HTTPS, and is used exclusively to fix bugs and improve app stability.

5. Third-Party Services & Payment Processing

Optional in-app upgrades (Pro Lifetime license) are processed directly and securely by Google Play In-App Billing. We never receive, process, or store your credit card details or financial information.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose utility tool suitable for all age groups.

7. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not collect, store, or process any personal data, we do not maintain personal databases. Users retain complete control over their physical storage devices and local data at all times.
